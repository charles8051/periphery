Whole-codebase sweep · 2026-06-20 · 6 parallel reviewers · ~42.5k LOC · 25 src projects + examples/tests/benchmarks

Two axes, reported together per finding: architecture (turn shallow modules into deep ones — leverage for callers, locality for maintainers, a real test surface) and standards (functional core / imperative shell, strongly-typed IDs, ADR conformance). Judged against the repo's own exemplar, the Treehopper pure core (ADR-0052). The diagrams carry the weight; prose stays in glossary terms.

Verdict up front. Periphery is a healthy codebase that already knows the functional-core/imperative-shell ideal and applies it well in its newest subsystems (Treehopper, the firmware-flashing platform). The friction is concentrated, not pervasive: two older state machines at the centre of the device model — DeviceTracker's latch resolution and DeviceProxyBase's recovery loop — are pure logic fused with their lock/clock/IO shell and tested past the interface, plus a handful of un-deepened seams and duplicated boilerplate.

Cross-cutting patterns

Six reviewers, independent lanes, converged on the same handful of shapes:

  • central Pure state machines fused with their lock/notify/Task shell, tested past the interface. The repo extracted pure decision values (RecoveryDirective, ResetStrategy, DeviceWaitState, AppReducer) but left its two biggest state machines fused — DeviceTracker is driven by 208 direct calls to its internal On* methods; DeviceProxyBase tangles decide/delay with reset IO.
  • recurring Buried clocks beside clock-injectable siblings. DeviceProxyBase reads Environment.TickCount64 and forces a pure backoff into an async signature it never awaits; CameraSession hard-wires Stopwatch/new CancellationTokenSource(timeout)/Task.Delay — while I2cDdcMonitorBackend, DeviceWaitState and the DFU poll do it right.
  • recurring Pure codec/parse fused into the IO shell. USB device-descriptor decode lives inside both backends with no pure parser (HID's HidReportDescriptor.Parse is the model); MegatecWire welds reassembly to its read loop; DeviceInfoTreeBuilder fuses presentation to Spectre.
  • recurring One unfinished typed ID at the centre. DeviceInfo.Id (and ParentId, SerialNumber) is a raw string while HardwareId/SerialPortName/UsbClassCode are typed record structs. Two reviewers flagged it from different clusters — it's the entity key threaded through every subsystem.
  • recurring Duplicated shallow boilerplate. Five identical per-enricher EnrichAsync shells, three structurally-identical DeviceProxy leaves, the CLI resolve+HID-error epilogue (×5-6), per-command parsers, FileLoggerProvider (×3), three first-match registries — and the pixel-format dimension math (×4, already drifted).
  • notable One purity leak in the exemplar itself. TreehopperWire.SpiClockByte reads Environment.GetEnvironmentVariable("TREEHOPPER_SPI_DANGER_BAND") on the pure SPI encode path — precisely the boundary ADR-0052 says "CI/code-review enforce."

The healthy grain to defend: the Treehopper pure core (ADR-0052); the firmware-flashing platform (DeviceWaitState, Stm32DfuPlan, Efm8BootRecordGenerator, AppReducer, ElfImage/IntelHexImage); Monitor's four pure cores and HID's Codecs/; the core value layer (immutable DeviceInfo, the typed IDs, DeviceFilter.Matches, DeviceInfoDiff); UsbDevice.RunTransferAsync; and the FlashAnything dual-mode host triad + the provider contract-test bases. New work should match these, not the two older state machines.

4.1 · Move the SPI danger-band env read out of the pure codec into the shell

Strongstandardsin-process

Problem. SpiClockByte reads Environment.GetEnvironmentVariable("TREEHOPPER_SPI_DANGER_BAND") (TreehopperWire.cs:455), and it's on Encode's path for every SpiTransaction.
